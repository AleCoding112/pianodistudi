Laurea magistrale, classe LM-Data. Accesso: libero. Lingua: Inglese.

Sito del corso

Il piano degli esami

1° anno

  • Programming and database — 12 CFU
  • Business organisation and management — 9 CFU
  • Ethics and law of data protection — 9 CFU
  • Probability for data science — 9 CFU

2° anno

  • Final exam — 21 CFU

In qualsiasi anno

  • (a) 1 module among the following (scegli 1 esame): Statistical models for data science 6 CFU; Continuous optimization for data science 6 CFU; Discrete optimization and decision making 6 CFU
  • (b) 2 courses among the following (a scelta): Data visualisation 6 CFU; Data security & privacy 6 CFU; Statistical learning 6 CFU; Mining massive datasets 6 CFU; Machine learning for data science 6 CFU
  • (c) 1 module among the following (scegli 1 esame): Statistical models for data science 6 CFU; Continuous optimization for data science 6 CFU; Discrete optimization and decision making 6 CFU; Complex systems and social physics (seminar course) 6 CFU; Network science and econophysics (seminar course) 6 CFU; Statistical methods for business intelligence (seminar course) 6 CFU
  • (d) 1 module among the following (scegli 1 esame): Cybercrime 6 CFU; Data protection in business organizations 6 CFU; Comparative and transnational law & technology 6 CFU; Social research 6 CFU; Big data epistemology 6 CFU
  • (e) 2 courses among the following (a scelta): Business analytics 6 CFU; Digital marketing and market research 6 CFU; Logistics, operations & supply chain 6 CFU; Digital transformation and it change 6 CFU; Marketing research for agrifood and natural resources 6 CFU
  • Activities to be chosen by the student (12 CFU during 1st and 2nd year) - 1st year - (scegli 12 CFU): Business analytics 6 CFU; Data visualisation 6 CFU; Data security & privacy 6 CFU; Statistical learning 6 CFU; Mining massive datasets 6 CFU; Machine learning for data science 6 CFU; Digital marketing and market research 6 CFU; Logistics, operations & supply chain 6 CFU; Digital transformation and it change 6 CFU; Statistical models for data science 6 CFU; Continuous optimization for data science 6 CFU; Discrete optimization and decision making 6 CFU; Marketing research for agrifood and natural resources 6 CFU; Cybercrime 6 CFU; Data protection in business organizations 6 CFU; Comparative and transnational law & technology 6 CFU; Social research 6 CFU; Big data epistemology 6 CFU; Complex systems and social physics (seminar course) 6 CFU; Network science and econophysics (seminar course) 6 CFU; Statistical methods for business intelligence (seminar course) 6 CFU
