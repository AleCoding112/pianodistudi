Il piano degli esami

2° anno

  • Final dissertation — 33 CFU

In qualsiasi anno

  • Earth materials: genesis, composition, evolution, properties — 6 CFU
  • Geological environments and structures — 6 CFU
  • Introduction to continuum physics — 6 CFU
  • Advanced topics in physics — 6 CFU
  • - The student must choose four of these courses, of which at least one for each of the GEOS-04/A, GEOS-04/B and GEOS-04/C subject areas (a scelta): Electrical, electromagnetic and gravimetric methods for environment and exploration 6 CFU; Physics of the atmosphere 6 CFU; Physics of the hydrosphere and the cryosphere 6 CFU; Seismic and wave field exploration 6 CFU; Seismology and laboratory 6 CFU; Solid earth geophysics 6 CFU
  • - The student must choose three of these courses (a scelta): Data analytics, forward and inverse modeling: electric and electromagnetic methods in applied geophysics 6 CFU; Data analytics, forward and inverse modeling: geophysical and environmental fluid dynamics 6 CFU; Environmental governance 6 CFU; Fisica dell'ambiente 6 CFU; Geodetic data modelling and inversion 6 CFU; Geostatistical methods for geophysics 6 CFU; International, european, and comparative environmental law 6 CFU; Metodi per l'elaborazione, analisi e rappresentazione di dati geofisici 6 CFU; Numerical modelling of geodynamic processes 6 CFU; Tectonophysics 6 CFU; The basics of probability theory and statistics 6 CFU
  • - The student must choose two of these courses and the other courses offered by the University (a scelta): Field course 6 CFU; Formation of stars and planets 6 CFU; Introduction to dynamic and synoptic meteorology 6 CFU; Seismic imaging 6 CFU; Spaceborne earth observation 6 CFU; Well logging 6 CFU
  • - Students are required to complete an internal or external internship for which they are awarded 9 CFU. Foreign students, who do not hold an Italian high school diploma, an Italian bachelor's degree or a certificate of knowledge at the A2 or higher level per the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), can acquire 3 CFU through the optional "Additional Language Skills: Italian" activity; in that case, they can complete an internship for which they are awarded 6 CFU. (scegli 9 CFU): Additional language skills: italian (3 ECTS) 3 CFU; Internship (6 ects) 6 CFU; Internship (9 ects) 9 CFU
