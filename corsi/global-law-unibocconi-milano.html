Global law — Università Bocconi, Milano

Laurea triennale, classe L-14. Accesso: numero programmato locale (posti limitati). Lingua: Inglese.

Sito del corso

Il piano degli esami

Percorsi (curricula): Major in Global Jurist, Major in Domestic Lawyer. Qui sotto: Major in Global Jurist.

1° anno

  • General Jurisprudence — 9 CFU
  • Critical thinking seminar — 1 CFU
  • Introduction to Private Law - Module 1 (Civil Law) — 9 CFU
  • Introduction to Private Law - Module 2 (Common Law) — 9 CFU
  • Transnational Constitutional Law - Module 1 (European Legal Systems) — 10 CFU
  • Transnational Constitutional Law - Module 2 (Anglo-American Legal Systems) — 8 CFU
  • Historical Foundations of Law - Module 1 (Civil law) — 6 CFU
  • Historical Foundations of Law - Module 2 (Common law) — 6 CFU

2° anno

  • European Union law — 9 CFU
  • Management of International Organizations — 6 CFU
  • Labour and Social Law — 6 CFU
  • International law — 9 CFU
  • Legal Tech — 3 CFU
  • Quantitative methods - preparatory — crediti non indicati
  • Global Administrative law — 8 CFU
  • Criminal Law — 10 CFU
  • EU language — 4 CFU
  • 1 course to be chosen from (scegli 1 esame): Microeconomics 6 CFU; Public Finance 6 CFU

3° anno

  • Quantitative analysis for global jurists — 6 CFU
  • Business and Corporate Law — 10 CFU
  • Final paper — 3 CFU
  • 1 course to be chosen from (scegli 1 esame): Global Civil Justice 6 CFU; Criminal Procedure 6 CFU
  • 1 course to be chosen from (scegli 1 esame): Economic Analysis of law 6 CFU; International Political Affairs 6 CFU
  • 1 course to be chosen from (scegli 1 esame): Administrative environmental law and climate change 6 CFU; Gender Law and women's rights 6 CFU
  • 1 course to be chosen from (scegli 1 esame): Regulatory law 6 CFU; Global Finance 6 CFU
  • 1 course to be chosen from (scegli 1 esame): Fundamentals of IT and IP Law 6 CFU; Sports law 6 CFU
  • Activity chosen by the student (scegli 6 CFU): Legal clinic (crediti non indicati); Drafting international contracts and emerging technologies (crediti non indicati); Internship (crediti non indicati)
  • Two seminars to be chosen among (scegli 2 esami): Cultural aspects of international negotiations (crediti non indicati); Democracy lab (crediti non indicati); Global diplomacy: mastering skills, strategies, and careers in international institutions (crediti non indicati)
