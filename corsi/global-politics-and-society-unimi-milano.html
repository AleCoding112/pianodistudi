Global Politics and Society — Università degli Studi di Milano, Milano

Laurea magistrale, classe LM-62. Accesso: libero. Lingua: Inglese.

Sito del corso

Il piano degli esami

1° anno

  • Global social dynamics — 9 CFU
  • Globalization and cultural diversity — 6 CFU
  • Multi-level legal system — 9 CFU
  • Comparative political economy and the welfare state — 12 CFU
  • Global social inequalities — 9 CFU
  • Students must earn 18 credits by selecting two of the following alternatives (scegli 18 CFU): Comparative politics and development 9 CFU; Economic and social regulation of global workforce 9 CFU; Global citizenship 9 CFU; Globalization, social justice and human rights 9 CFU; Human rights at work in international and comparative law 9 CFU; International migration and immigration policies 9 CFU

2° anno

  • Global politics — 9 CFU
  • Final exam — 15 CFU
  • Students must earn 18 credits by selecting three of the following alternatives (scegli 18 CFU): Consumer culture 6 CFU; Datafied society 6 CFU; Employment, inequalities and discrimination in the global labour market 6 CFU; Gender theory and social issues 6 CFU; Global challenges to democracy 6 CFU; Global public policy and the knowledge society 6 CFU; Journalism,media and politics 6 CFU; Labour markets and globalization 6 CFU; Liberal rights and social justice 6 CFU; Migrations, diversity and non-discrimination 6 CFU; Social justice in a global world 6 CFU; The welfare state and social innovation 6 CFU; Value change and global societies 6 CFU

In qualsiasi anno

  • - Student must earn 6 credits by selecting among the following activities: Internship (6 or 3 ECTS), Additional/advanced Language skills (3 ECTS) and Laboratories and other activities (3 ECTS) (scegli 6 CFU): Additional language skills: italian (3 ECTS) 3 CFU; Advanced language skills: french (3 ECTS) 3 CFU; Advanced language skills: german (3 ECTS) 3 CFU; Advanced language skills: spanish (3 ECTS) 3 CFU; Data lab 3 CFU; Dissertation lab 3 CFU; Lab. contentious citizenship and global seams 3 CFU; Lab. qualitative methods and empirical research: space, place and mobility 3 CFU
  • - Students must earn 9 credits for elective activities Among these activities, students can choose also "Research methods in social and political science" (scegli 9 CFU): Research methods in social and political science 9 CFU
  • Attività a scelta dello studente (facoltativo): Lab. studying cultural change with quantitative methods 3 CFU; Lab. global health inequalities: analysis and perspectives 3 CFU
