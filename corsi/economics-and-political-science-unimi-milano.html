Sito del corso

Il piano degli esami

1° anno

  • Game theory — 6 CFU
  • Mathematical methods — 6 CFU
  • Statistics, econometrics and logic for economic and political sciences — 12 CFU
  • Advanced microeconomics — 6 CFU
  • Advanced macroeconomics — 6 CFU
  • Comparative politics — 9 CFU
  • International economic law — 6 CFU
  • - Students must earn 9 credits by selecting one of the following alternatives: (scegli 9 CFU): Comparative welfare states 9 CFU; Empirical methods for economics and policy evaluation 9 CFU

2° anno

  • Firms in international and developing markets — 12 CFU
  • Positive political theory — 9 CFU
  • Final exam — 15 CFU
  • - Students must earn 6 credits by selecting one of the following alternatives: (scegli 6 CFU): Governance of the european union 6 CFU; Social justice in a global world 6 CFU
  • Insegnamenti facoltativi (facoltativo): Dynamic macroeconomics 6 CFU

In qualsiasi anno

  • - Students must earn 15 credits for elective activities. Second year students may choose, among others, the following elective activity for 6 credits: (scegli 15 CFU): Dynamic macroeconomics 6 CFU
  • - Students must earn 3 credits by selecting one of the following alternatives: Foreign Language - advanced; Computer skills - advanced; Internship; Laboratory; Orientation Seminar. (scegli 3 CFU): Additional language skills: italian (3 ECTS) 3 CFU; Advanced computer skills 3 CFU; Advanced language skills: french (3 ECTS) 3 CFU; Advanced language skills: german (3 ECTS) 3 CFU; Advanced language skills: spanish (3 ECTS) 3 CFU; Laboratory: introduction to coding and data management for economics and political science 3 CFU; Orientation seminar 3 CFU
  • Attività a scelta dello studente (facoltativo): Laboratory: public policy in government 3 CFU
