1° anno

  • Coding Theory and Applications — 6 CFU
  • Computational algebra — 6 CFU
  • Public-key and post-quantum cryptography — 6 CFU
  • Symmetric cryptography and finite fields — 6 CFU
  • Ulteriori conoscenze linguistiche (inglese C1) — 3 CFU
  • Scegliere 15 CFU tra i seguenti insegnamenti (offerti al primo e al secondo anno) (15 CFU) (scegli 15 CFU): Mathematical Physics - Quantum Relativistic Theories 9 CFU; Quantum information 9 CFU; Scientific computing 9 CFU; Statistics of Stochastic Processes 6 CFU; Stochastic Processes 9 CFU; Stochastic processes (I modulo) 6 CFU

(36 CFU) (scegli 36 CFU): Advanced Number Theory 6 CFU; Advanced Programming of Cryptographic Methods 6 CFU; Advanced Public-key Cryptography 6 CFU; Advanced Symmetric Cryptography 6 CFU; Algebraic geometry I 6 CFU; Applied Cryptography 6 CFU; Automated Reasoning and Formal Verification 12 CFU; Blockchain 6 CFU; Computability and computational complexity 6 CFU; Cryptographic Protocols for Secure Networks and Applications 6 CFU; Digital signal processing 6 CFU; Discrete Fourier analysis 6 CFU; Introduction to Computer and Network Security 6 CFU

  • Scegliere tra una tesi da 30 CFU e un'attività di tirocinio/stage (12 CFU) seguita da una tesi da 18 CFU (30 CFU) (scegli 30 CFU): Stage/Internship 12 CFU; Thesis 18 CFU; Thesis 30 CFU
