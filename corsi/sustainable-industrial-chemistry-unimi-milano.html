Laurea magistrale, classe LM-71. Accesso: libero. Lingua: Inglese.

Sito del corso

Il piano degli esami

1° anno

  • Advanced industrial chemistry with lab — 9 CFU
  • Chemical processes and industrial plants — 6 CFU
  • Economics and management — 6 CFU
  • TABLE 1 - ELECTIVE 9 CFU COURSES Student must earn 9 credits by selecting 1 of the following courses (scegli 9 CFU): Advanced functional materials for industrial applications with lab 9 CFU; Applied organic chemistry with lab 9 CFU; Chemical technologies for the energy transition with lab 9 CFU

2° anno

  • Experimental research stage — 15 CFU
  • Thesis work and final dissertation — 24 CFU
  • STUDENTS MUST ACQUIRE 3 CREDITS according to the following rules: 1) students holding an Italian high school diploma or university degree must obtain 3 credits in Chemical regulation and legislation; 2) students not holding an Italian high school diploma or university degree must obtain 3 credits ALTERNATIVELY in Chemical regulation and legislation or in Additional language skills: Italian. More information is provided in the "Language test" paragraph above. (scegli 3 CFU): Additional language skills: italian (3 ECTS) 3 CFU; Chemical regulation and legislation 3 CFU

In qualsiasi anno

  • TABLE 2 - ELECTIVE 6 CFU COURSES A Student must earn 6 credits by selecting 1 of the following courses (scegli 6 CFU): Advanced methods in organic synthesis 6 CFU; Analytics for chemical industry 6 CFU; Catalytic methodologies in organic synthesis 6 CFU; Concepts and methods in organic synthesis 6 CFU; Environmental control and sustainability management 6 CFU; Environmental electrochemistry 6 CFU; Formulation science and technology 6 CFU; Heterogeneous catalysis 6 CFU; Machine learning for chemical sciences and industry 6 CFU; Metal science and corrosion 6 CFU; Nanotechnology for advanced materials 6 CFU; Polymorphism and crystal forms in industry 6 CFU; Recovery and recycling of critical materials and chemicals for waste management 6 CFU; Sustainable synthetic methodologies in homogeneous catalysis 6 CFU; Synthetic methods in biotechnology 6 CFU; Technology-driven organic synthesis 6 CFU
  • TABLE 3 - ELECTIVE 6 CFU COURSES B Student must earn 12 credits by selecting 2 courses from TABLE 2 or TABLE 3 (scegli 6 CFU): Plastics degradation and its environmental impact 6 CFU; Polymer testing and analysis 6 CFU; Process development 6 CFU; Recycle and life cycle assessment (LCA) of products and processes 6 CFU
  • TABLE 4 - ELECTIVE 6 CFU COURSES C Student must earn 6 credits by selecting 1 course from TABLE 4 (scegli 6 CFU): Chemistry digitalization for industry 4.0 6 CFU; Design and optimization of chemical plants 6 CFU; Fermentation biotechnology 6 CFU; Industrial processes and scale-up 6 CFU; Medicinal chemistry 6 CFU; Patents and management of innovation 6 CFU
  • TABLE 5 Student must earn 12 credits by selecting 2 related and integrative courses from TABLE 5 (scegli 12 CFU): (bio)nanotechnology 6 CFU; C language programming 6 CFU; Chemical safety 6 CFU; Chemometrics 6 CFU; Energy economics 6 CFU; Information technology 6 CFU; Programming for chemistry 6 CFU; Protein biochemistry 6 CFU; Protein engineering and molecular enzymology 6 CFU; Technological properties of minerals, cements and ceramics 6 CFU
