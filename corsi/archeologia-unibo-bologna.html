Il piano degli esami

Curricula: Curriculum Applied Critical Archaeology and Heritage: per studenti immatricolati nell'a.a. 2026/27, Curriculum Archeologia e Culture del Mondo Antico: per studenti immatricolati nell'a.a. 2026/27. Qui sotto: Curriculum Applied Critical Archaeology and Heritage: per studenti immatricolati nell'a.a. 2026/27.

1° anno

  • Archaeological Theory and Practice — 12 CFU
  • Two Elective Units in History (12 CFU) (scegli 12 CFU): Civilizations of the Ancient Near East 6 CFU; Cross-Cultural Encounters in the Medieval World 6 CFU; Ethnicity and Identity in the Greek World (1) 6 CFU; Rome and the Universal 6 CFU
  • Two Elective Units in Monuments and Memory in Archaeology (12 CFU) (scegli 12 CFU): Archaeology of Monuments and Memory in Central Asia 6 CFU; Archaeology of Monuments and Memory in the Ancient Near East 6 CFU; Funerary Landscape in the Etruscan World 6 CFU; Monuments, Matter, and Memory in Ancient Mesoamerica 6 CFU; Museum Studies in Archaeology 6 CFU; Public Archaeology and Memory 6 CFU
  • Two Elective Units in Social Approaches to Archaeology (12 CFU) (scegli 12 CFU): Environment, Society, and Landscapes of Medieval Italy 6 CFU; Household Archaeology in the Mediterranean 6 CFU; Late Prehistory in the Mediterranean 6 CFU; The Archaeology of the Roman Economy 6 CFU
  • One Elective Unit in Interdisciplinary Approaches to Heritage (6 CFU) (scegli 6 CFU): Cultural Policy and Comparative Heritage Legislation 6 CFU; Scientific Methods for Exploring Archaeological Records 6 CFU
  • Fieldwork and Laboratory — 6 CFU

2° anno

  • One Elective Unit in Methodology in Archaeology (6 CFU) (scegli 6 CFU): Archaeology of Disasters and Climate Change 6 CFU; Digital Archaeology 6 CFU; Networks And Mobility in Archaeology 6 CFU
  • Two Elective Units in Material and Written Culture (12 CFU) (scegli 12 CFU): Archaeology of the Body in Roman and Post-Classical Worlds 6 CFU; European Protohistory 6 CFU; Iconography and Iconology Between Ancient Greece and Etruria 6 CFU; Iron Age Material Culture and Production 6 CFU; Lithic Technology in Prehistory 6 CFU; Cuneiform Script and the Archaeology of Writing in Ancient Mesopotamia 6 CFU; Provenance and Contextualisation of Papyrus Findings 6 CFU
  • Seminars in Archaeological Practice and Heritage — 6 CFU
  • One elective among the following list (6 CFU) (scegli 6 CFU): Fieldwork and Laboratory 6 CFU; Internship 6 CFU
  • Eligible Courses among Unibo Teachings (12 - 48 CFU) (scegli tra 12 e 48 CFU):
  • Final Examination (18 CFU) (scegli 18 CFU): Final examination (dissertation) 18 CFU; Final examination (dissertation) 12 CFU; Preparation for the final examination abroad 6 CFU; Final examination (dissertation) 6 CFU; Internship abroad for preparation for the final examination 6 CFU; Preparation for the final examination abroad 6 CFU
