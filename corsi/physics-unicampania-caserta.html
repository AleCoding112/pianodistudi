Il piano degli esami

1° anno

  • Physics Laboratory — 10 CFU
  • Computational Methods for Physics — 9 CFU
  • Further linguistic knowledge (Italian Course for non-italian citizens OR Scientific English) — 4 CFU
  • Elective courses from Group 1 (Teorico e dei fondamenti della fisica) (scegli 2 esami): Theoretical Physics 8 CFU; Models and tools for weather prediction and climate 8 CFU; Statistical Mechanics 8 CFU; Theory of Quantum Computation 8 CFU
  • Elective courses from Group 2 (Microfisico e della struttura della materia) (scegli 2 esami): Nuclear and Subnuclear Physics 6 CFU; Quantum Electronics 6 CFU; Isotope physics and methods 6 CFU; Condensed Matter Physics 6 CFU; Stochastic processes 6 CFU
  • 1 Elective course from Group 3 (affini o integrative) (scegli 1 esame): Introduction to Optical sensing techniques 6 CFU; Astrophysics 6 CFU; Ecological climatology 6 CFU; Applied Biophotonics 6 CFU; Complementary topics of theoretical physics 6 CFU; Radiation Biophysics 6 CFU; Aerospace physics & methodologies 6 CFU; Photonics and Nanotechnologies 6 CFU; Modeling of complex systems 6 CFU

2° anno

  • Tirocinio — 3 CFU
  • Prova finale (tesi) — 42 CFU
  • Second elective course from Group 3 (affini o integrative) (scegli 1 esame): Photonics and Nanotechnologies 6 CFU; Modeling of complex systems 6 CFU; Introduction to Optical sensing techniques 6 CFU; Astrophysics 6 CFU; Ecological climatology 6 CFU; Applied Biophotonics 6 CFU; Complementary topics of theoretical physics 6 CFU; Radiation Biophysics 6 CFU; Aerospace physics & methodologies 6 CFU
  • 2 corsi a scelta libera (TAF-D), Group 4 o corsi non scelti dei Group 1-3 (scegli 2 esami): Advanced experimental techniques for nuclear and particle physics 6 CFU; Laser spectroscopy 6 CFU; Particle astrophysics 6 CFU; Microscopic Nuclear Structure 6 CFU; Physics for Archaeology and Cultural heritage 6 CFU; Astrophysical Techniques 6 CFU; Experimental Gravitation 6 CFU; Physics of the Sun and of the heliosphere 6 CFU; Astrophysics of Galaxies 6 CFU; Advanced and Computational Solid State Physics 6 CFU; Aerospace Propulsion and plasma physics 6 CFU; Space access and Earth observation 6 CFU; Machine Learning and AI (First Part) 6 CFU; Advanced Operational Research 6 CFU; Network Analysis 6 CFU; Numerical Models in Data Processing 6 CFU; Machine Learning and AI (Second Part) 6 CFU
