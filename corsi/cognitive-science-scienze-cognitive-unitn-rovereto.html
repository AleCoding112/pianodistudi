Cognitive Science - Scienze Cognitive — Università degli Studi di Trento, Rovereto

Laurea magistrale, classe LM-55. Accesso: programmato locale. Lingua: Inglese.

Sito del corso

Il piano degli esami

Curricula: Fundamental behavioural neuroscience, Computational and theoretical modelling of language and cognition, Cognitive Neuroscience. Qui sotto: Fundamental behavioural neuroscience.

1° anno

  • Animal Cognition and Neuroscience — 9 CFU
  • Foundations of Cognitive Psychology and Neuroscience I — 6 CFU
  • Introduction to Computer Programming for FBN (Matlab) — 6 CFU
  • Neural Foundations of Human Behavior — 6 CFU
  • Elective Courses 1 - Fundamental Behavioural Neuroscience (2 attività didattiche) (scegli 2 esami): Cellular and Molecular Neuroscience 9 CFU; Cognitive neuroscience of infant development 6 CFU; Comparative neuroanatomy and evolution 6 CFU; Invertebrate Neuroscience 6 CFU
  • Elective Courses 2 - Fundamental Behavioural Neuroscience (1 attività didattica) (scegli 1 esame): Advanced Statistical Methods 9 CFU; Introduction to Statistical Methods 9 CFU
  • Elective Courses 3 - Fundamental Behavioural Neuroscience (1 attività didattica) (scegli 1 esame): Brain Asymmetries in Language and Cognition 6 CFU; Introduction to Human Language 6 CFU
  • Free choice courses - Fundamental Behavioural Neuroscience (fra 3 e 15 CFU) (scegli tra 3 e 15 CFU): Advanced Hands on M/EEG Analysis 6 CFU; Advanced Hands on fMRI Analysis 6 CFU; Advanced Topics in Motor Cognition 6 CFU; Advanced topics in reasoning and decision making 6 CFU; Affective Neuroscience 6 CFU; Brain Stimulation and Multimodal Electrophysiological Recording 6 CFU; Bridging to Cognitive Neuroscience 3 CFU; Bridging to Fundamental Behavioural Neuroscience 3 CFU; Cognitive Impairment and Dementia 6 CFU; Language and Social Cognition 6 CFU; Language modeling and human cognition 6 CFU; Methods in Comparative Neuroanatomy 3 CFU; Predictive Processing in the Brain: Perception and Action 3 CFU; Project-Based EEG Laboratory 6 CFU

2° anno

  • Brain Development and Disease — 9 CFU
  • Internship — 15 CFU
  • Master Thesis — 30 CFU
